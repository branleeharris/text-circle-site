Text Circle

Write about the day’s text, together.

Read the day’s text, then write a few lines about it. Your family and close friends do the same, and everyone in your circle sees who has written today and how many days in a row you’ve kept it up.

  • One entry a day, shared with every circle you’re in
  • Your streak and your circle’s streak, with milestones from a week to a year
  • Several circles: family, friends, anyone you invite
  • Reminders at the time you usually write

No accounts and no servers: circles are shared through iCloud, only between the people you invite. For iPhone and iPad.
