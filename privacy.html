Text Circle

Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Text Circle has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you write goes to your iCloud and to the people in your circles, and nowhere else. We (the developer) can’t see it.

What the app keeps

  • What you write: your entry for each day, the scripture you type with it, when you wrote it and your time zone (so others see the time on your clock).
  • Your name and picture, as you set them in the app.
  • Your circles: their names and colors, and who is in them.
  • A place, only if you add one: the city you’re writing from (for example “Lisbon, Portugal”).

All of this is stored on your device and in your iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit. Each circle is shared, through iCloud, only with the people its starter invited. Apple stores and protects this data under the Apple Privacy Policy. The developer has no access to it.

Location

Text Circle asks for your location only when you choose to add a place to an entry, and it asks for approximate location only. Your device turns it into a city name using Apple’s map services; only the city name is kept and shared with your circles. Coordinates are never saved or sent. You can type a place instead, or leave it out.

Notifications

Reminders to write are scheduled on your device. When someone in your circle writes, iCloud tells your device so it can show a notification. No notification content passes through any server of ours.

The day’s text

Text Circle doesn’t include or download the day’s text. The Read button opens it in JW Library or on jw.org, whose own privacy policies apply there.

Removing your data

  • Delete an entry from Today or the calendar; it’s removed from every circle.
  • Leave a circle, or, if you started it, delete it for everyone, from the circle’s page.
  • To remove everything, delete the app, then in Settings go to your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Text Circle and choose Delete Data.

Reports

If you report an entry from inside the app, it opens an email to us with the entry’s text and the name of its writer. We use it only to look into the report.

Children

Text Circle isn’t directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
