Starting a circle and inviting people

Tap the Circle button at the top of Today, then New Circle. Give it a name and a color, then tap Invite or Manage People to send a link by Messages, Mail or any app. Whoever opens the link on an iPhone or iPad with Undertones joins the circle. Everyone needs to be signed in to iCloud.

How streaks count

A day counts when you first write before midnight on your own clock. You can still write about a day you missed from Past Days (the calendar button on Today), but it won’t count toward a streak. A circle’s streak is the days everyone in it wrote.

I don’t see someone’s entry

  • Pull down on Today to check for new entries.
  • Make sure both of you are signed in to iCloud and that iCloud Drive is on.
  • If an invitation didn’t open the app, open it again from Messages after installing Undertones.

Reminders

Undertones reminds you an hour after the time you usually write, at 9 PM when your circle’s streak is waiting on you, and at 11 PM as a last call. Turn them off under Reminders on the Circle page (the Circle button at the top of Today).

Reporting or hiding someone

Touch and hold an entry to report it or to hide everything from its writer on this device. Whoever started a circle can also remove people from it under Circle › Invite or Manage People.

Leaving, deleting and your data

Leave a circle from the bottom of the Circle page; if you started it, you can delete it for everyone. See the privacy policy for how to remove everything from iCloud.
